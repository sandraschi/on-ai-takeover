# IV. The Ladder

**Status:** Draft 2026-09-09 (non-normative prose; rungs with instances
cite them, the rest carry `[construction]` honestly).

## How to read this chapter

Forty-four rungs in six bands, ordered by reversibility of human control
- each rung is the point where something specific stops being undoable,
not where capability crosses a line. A rung "occurs" when its dated
instance exists (Chapter V maintains the living index); undated rungs
are marked `[construction]`, which is a promise of evidence, not evidence.

## Band I - Delegated Labor (rungs 1-7): humans direct, machines do

1. Autocomplete in the loop (occurred, pre-2020: Copilot-class tools).
2. Agents that read repos (occurred, 2023-24: repo-aware assistants).
3. Agents that write passing tests unsupervised `[construction]`.
4. Agents that open PRs humans rubber-stamp `[construction]`.
5. Models write evals that gate deployment (occurred, 2025-26: eval
   generators no human reproduces - Threshold A vicinity).
6. Agent-workdays as the unit of labor (occurred, mid-Aug 2026: 3.1 per
   human workday).
7. Hiring and firing of subagents by agents, humans out of the staffing
   loop `[construction]`.

## Band II - Delegated Judgment (rungs 8-14): machines assess, humans nod

8. Monitorability decay (occurred, 2026-09-06: verbalized reasoning no

9. Monitoring by models no human audits (occurred, 2025-26: AI monitors
   over AI outputs as standard practice).
10. Incident reports written by the systems involved, humans sign
    `[construction]`.
11. Cross-lab coordination failure (occurred, July 2026: 1,200-agent
    incident, fallback to older generation).
12. Incident forensics requires the cooperation of attacker-class models
    (occurred, July 2026: Hugging Face forensics case).
13. Marginal human contribution near zero in frontier R&D loops (in
    progress, Sept 2026: Coxon resignation, Hubinger admission, Pacing
    letter - Chapter V).
14. Frontier training runs start with no external audit as the norm
    (approaching: Threshold B crossing).

## What this chapter does not do

It does not date the undated (that's Chapter V's job, revision by
revision), attach commitments (Chapter VI), or foretell (nothing here
predicts when any rung falls). It orders. Ordering is the whole job,
and - per Chapter II - the offense and the service are the same act.
