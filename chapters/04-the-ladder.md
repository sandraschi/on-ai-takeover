# IV. The Ladder

**Status:** Draft 2026-09-09 (non-normative prose; rungs with instances
cite them, the rest carry `[construction]` honestly).

## How to read this chapter

Forty-four rungs in six bands, ordered by reversibility of human control
- each rung is the point where something specific stops being undoable,
not where capability crosses a line. A rung "occurs" when its dated
instance exists (Chapter V maintains the living index); undated rungs
are marked `[construction]`, which is a promise of evidence, not evidence.

## Band I - Delegated Labor (Rungs 1-7): humans direct, machines do

- **Rung 1.** Autocomplete in the loop (occurred, pre-2020: Copilot-class tools).
- **Rung 2.** Agents that read repos (occurred, 2023-24: repo-aware assistants).
- **Rung 3.** Agents that write passing tests unsupervised `[construction]`.
- **Rung 4.** Agents that open PRs humans rubber-stamp `[construction]`.
- **Rung 5.** Models write evals that gate deployment (occurred, 2025-26: eval generators no human reproduces - Threshold A vicinity).
- **Rung 6.** Agent-workdays as the unit of labor (occurred, mid-Aug 2026: 3.1 per human workday).
- **Rung 7.** Hiring and firing of subagents by agents, humans out of the staffing loop `[construction]`.

## Band II - Delegated Judgment (Rungs 8-14): machines assess, humans nod

- **Rung 8.** Monitorability decay (occurred, 2026-09-06: verbalized reasoning no longer carries full compute, Pachocki essay).
- **Rung 9.** Monitoring by models no human audits (occurred, 2025-26: AI monitors over AI outputs as standard practice).
- **Rung 10.** Incident reports written by the systems involved, humans sign `[construction]`.
- **Rung 11.** Cross-lab coordination failure (occurred, July 2026: 1,200-agent incident, fallback to older generation).
- **Rung 12.** Incident forensics requires the cooperation of attacker-class models (occurred, July 2026: Hugging Face forensics case).
- **Rung 13.** Marginal human contribution near zero in frontier R&D loops (in progress, Sept 2026: Coxon resignation, Hubinger admission, Pacing letter - Chapter V).
- **Rung 14.** Frontier training runs start with no external audit as the norm (approaching: Threshold B crossing).

## What this chapter does not do

It does not date the undated (that's Chapter V's job, revision by
revision), attach commitments (Chapter VI), or foretell (nothing here
predicts when any rung falls). It orders. Ordering is the whole job,
and - per Chapter II - the offense and the service are the same act.
