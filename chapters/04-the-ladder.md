# IV. The Ladder

**Status:** Stub — compact version live in README, this chapter is the expanded, fully-cited version.

## Scope

The full forty-four rungs, six bands, five named thresholds. Every rung
gets its observable, its citation (or an explicit "construction, unevidenced"
flag), and the argument for its position in the reversibility-of-control
ordering. This is the centrepiece chapter and the one where "every rung is
an observable fact" (CONTRIBUTING.md, Rule 1) actually gets tested
forty-four times.

## Current evidence status (2026-09-08)

Rungs with a real citation: **1, 6, 8, 11, 13**. Everything else in Bands
III through VI is asserted, not evidenced — that's the main work of this
chapter, not a footnote to it.
