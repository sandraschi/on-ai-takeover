# VIII. Descent

**Status:** Draft 2026-09-09 (non-normative prose).

## Scope discipline, repeated because it matters most here

This chapter names descent conditions - institutional commitments that
move a civilization back below a threshold. It does not design fallback
systems. "Retained redundant human capability" is a governance stance;
step-by-step architecture of a specific fallback crosses into territory
Rule 2 rules out (CONTRIBUTING.md). Kahn kept de-escalation rungs named,
verifiable, and policy-shaped. So does this chapter.

## Why the literature is empty here

Volumes exist on escalation risk and almost nothing on recovery - because
escalation has vendors (labs sell capability, critics sell alarm) while
descent has no constituency: nobody's budget grows when a model
generation retires, nobody keynotes redundancy. The asymmetry is the
finding. Descent must be built before it is needed, by people who will
not be rewarded for it, which is why it needs thresholds to attach to
rather than goodwill to hope for.

## Descent conditions per threshold (Chapter VI)

- **Below E:** none realistic. Say so explicitly: once rebuild time
  exceeds decision cycles with no path back, no commitment restores it.
  E is a cliff, not a slope. The policy content of E is entirely
  pre-emptive - the drill requirement before retirement, never after.
- **Below D:** provenance enforcement restored at the platform layer,
  lapsed powers returned with published accounting, elections observed
  to function. Partially realistic while institutions still meet.
- **Below C:** shutdown path re-tested under observation, exercise logs
  published, next run gated on passage. Realistic wherever physical
  infrastructure still answers to humans.
- **Below B:** run cards and generation times published retroactively
  for unaudited runs, external audit regime chartered with access rights.
  Realistic - it is paperwork plus access, and paperwork is cheap.
- **Below A:** eval generators reproduced independently, lineage
  published. The cheapest descent on the list; its absence measures will.

## Retirement discipline

The quiet engine of Band III accumulation is default: nothing retires
unless someone pays the cost of retiring it, so dependence accrues while
attention is elsewhere. The corresponding commitment is procedural, not
heroic: every frontier deployment carries a retirement date and a named
owner of the shutdown, reviewed annually. Systems without owners do not
get deployed. That sentence, enforced, prevents half of Band III.
