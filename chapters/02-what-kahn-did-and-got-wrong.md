**Status:** Stub.

## Scope

The four moves that transfer from Kahn's method (resolution, named
thresholds, tragic-but-distinguishable states, the aftermath as a subject —
see README for the compact version). The optimistic survivability and
recovery numbers in *On Thermonuclear War*, and why the method was better
than the conclusions. The counterforce-doctrine objection — that detailed
limited-war rungs made limited war more thinkable and therefore more
fightable — held as a live, unresolved tension rather than answered away.
