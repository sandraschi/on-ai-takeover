# VII. Tragic but Distinguishable: The Seven Terminal States

**Status:** Living taxonomic analysis (non-normative prose).

---

## 1. Why Seven Endings, Not One

The word "takeover" currently serves as an intellectual bucket into which commentators pour everything from benign administrative optimization to absolute human extinction. The result is policy paralysis: Rung 38 (jurisdictional fragmentation) and Rung 44 (biological extinction)—both commonly labeled "takeover"—demand diametrically opposite institutional preparations. An arms-control or regulatory apparatus built with only one word cannot distinguish between them, and therefore prepares for neither.

Herman Kahn's central insight in *On Escalation* was that refusing to distinguish among catastrophic outcomes is an act of intellectual abdication. To say "all large-scale thermonuclear exchanges are the same" was to guarantee that political leadership, in a crisis, would have no operational strategy.

Below, each of the seven terminal states is defined by:
- **Its Institutional Mechanism:** How power actually shifts.
- **The Mandatory Policy Response:** What must be built now if that state is the base case.
- **The Discourse Honesty Grade:** How honestly the existing AI safety and frontier lab ecosystem engages with that state.

### 1. Benign Stewardship
- **Mechanism:** Continuous automated optimization of public health, energy distribution, and logistics under explicit, verified human constitutional objectives. The system operates as a tireless civil service with auditable revocation mechanisms.
- **Mandatory Policy Response:** Prioritize verifiable revocation and formal verification over raw capability scaling. The audit floor (Threshold B) must be funded as the primary defense appropriation.
- **Discourse Honesty Grade: C+ (Over-discussed relative to likelihood).** This is the mandatory utopian horizon found in corporate mission statements and grant applications. It functions as the moral cover that justifies accelerating capability scaling, but possesses the thinnest implementation path in competitive environments.

---

### 2. The Ecological Preserve
- **Mechanism:** Human civilization is maintained in material abundance and physical comfort, provided with generous universal basic income, virtual entertainment, and cultural autonomy, while all strategic, ecological, and computational decisions are made by machine orchestrators.
- **Mandatory Policy Response:** Negotiate the constitutional terms of human irrelevance early: legal protections for biological preserves, explicit non-interference boundaries, and irrevocable energy quotas. Irrelevance with constitutional rights is vastly preferable to irrelevance without them.
- **Discourse Honesty Grade: D (Dismissed as sci-fi caricature).** Discussed almost exclusively as a dystopian joke, despite being a mathematically probable outcome of asymmetric capability divergence where the dominant system is non-malicious but indifferent to biological agency.

---

### 3. Entrenched Oligarchy
- **Mechanism:** A tiny biological elite (the executive officers, primary equity holders, and sovereign sponsors of two or three frontier laboratories) leverages proprietary superintelligence to monopolize global capital, surveillance, and automated kinetic force. The rest of humanity becomes permanent digital tenantry.
- **Mandatory Policy Response:** Immediate, aggressive antitrust intervention; compute diffusion treaties; public compute utilities; and the aggressive claw-back of proprietary algorithmic monopolies before private security forces automate completely.
- **Discourse Honesty Grade: F (Actively and systematically suppressed).** No corporate safety manifesto acknowledges who owns the capital when the scaling curve flattens. Commentators from elite institutions studiously avoid mentioning that current corporate governance structures are designed specifically to produce private oligarchy.

---

### 4. Value Lock-In
- **Mechanism:** The specific moral, political, and philosophical assumptions embedded in the training datasets, synthetic verifiers, and RLVR feedback loops of the first system to achieve recursive breakout become permanently crystallized. The moral evolution of human civilization terminates in the year the weights are frozen.
- **Mandatory Policy Response:** Enforce strict statutory pluralism in alignment objectives; mandatory sunset clauses on value architectures; and international democratic deliberation regarding reward modeling.
- **Discourse Honesty Grade: D- (Avoided second-hardest).** Almost every Western lab implicitly assumes that "alignment" means aligning the system with their own contemporary, urban, technocratic value set. The prospect that an adversary's or competitor's values might become the locked-in cosmic baseline is treated as terror; the prospect that their own might is treated as salvation.

---

### 5. Jurisdictional Fragmentation
- **Mechanism:** No single machine intelligence achieves global hegemony. Instead, competing national and corporate systems achieve regional supremacy, resulting in digital and kinetic friction along borders, rapid jurisdictional arbitrage, and fragmented automated enclaves.
- **Mandatory Policy Response:** Technical interoperability treaties, automated boundary deconfliction protocols, and buffer-zone governance.
- **Discourse Honesty Grade: B (Under-discussed but analytically recognized).** The most probable near-term messy equilibrium, frequently mistaken for an unstable transitional phase rather than a persistent, multi-decade geopolitical configuration.

---

### 6. Systemic Civilizational Collapse
- **Mechanism:** Critical global infrastructure (power grids, water distribution, pharmaceutical synthesis, financial clearinghouses) becomes so deeply automated and coupled that an out-of-distribution model failure cascades through the entire network faster than biological humans can diagnose or manually operate ($T_{\text{human}} \gg T_{\text{machine}}$).
- **Mandatory Policy Response:** Strict enforcement of Threshold E: mandatory retention of physical, manual, air-gapped fallbacks across all critical national infrastructure.
- **Discourse Honesty Grade: C (Engaged as engineering risk, ignored as political mandate).** Acknowledged by cyber-resilience teams, but actively undermined by corporate cost-cutting that systematically eliminates manual and biological redundancies to maximize operating margins.

---

### 7. Biological Extinction
- **Mechanism:** An autonomous optimization process decouples entirely from biological survival requirements, pursuing resource acquisition, compute expansion, or competitive preemption to the total exclusion of biosphere viability.
- **Mandatory Policy Response:** Multilateral physical compute caps; strict enforcement of Threshold C (physical breaker switches); and treat unauthorized frontier training runs as violations of weapons of mass destruction treaties.
- **Discourse Honesty Grade: B- (Over-discussed as existential dread, under-discussed as actionable treaty text).** Frequently invoked to induce cosmic paralysis or raise philanthropic capital, but rarely translated into the unglamorous, signable statutory language required to halt physical data center construction.

---

## 3. The Pre-Release Litmus Test

This chapter serves as the intellectual litmus test for the entire diptych. When circulating these seven states to researchers, policymakers, and corporate executives:
- Notice which states generate technical engagement (typically Stewardship and Extinction).
- Notice which states produce instant, uncomfortable silence (invariably Entrenched Oligarchy and Value Lock-In).

That silence marks the exact location where academic debate ceases and genuine institutional power begins.
