# VII. Tragic but Distinguishable

**Status:** Stub.

## Scope

The seven terminal states, at length: benign stewardship, the preserve,
entrenched oligarchy, value lock-in, fragmentation, collapse, extinction.
Why flattening these into one word ("takeover") disarms policy, since
rungs 38 and 44 are both called takeover under current usage but call for
opposite responses. Kahn's most hated and most important claim, applied
here: refusing to distinguish between bad outcomes is not moral
seriousness, it's moral abdication.
