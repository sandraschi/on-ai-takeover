# III. No Equation of State

**Status:** Stub.

## Scope

Kahn had physics: yields, blast radii, fallout deposition curves, industrial
recovery models. He could compute, and critics could check the computation.
There is no equation of state for AI loss of control. This chapter states
that absence plainly as the real reason nobody has written this book, and
derives the one design constraint that follows from it: every rung must be
defined by an observable act or an observable loss of institutional
capability, never by an inferred intention.

## Notes toward a draft

- This is the chapter that has to earn the "observable, not inferred"
