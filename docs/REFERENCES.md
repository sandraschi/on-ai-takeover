## Epistemic Grading Taxonomy

Every source cited across the ten chapters of `on-ai-takeover` is evaluated according to a three-tier epistemic hierarchy to prevent speculative drift:

* **Tier 1 (Primary Admissions & Empirical Telemetry):** Direct statements on the public record by laboratory leadership, sworn testimony, corporate disclosures, SEC filings, verified cryptographic run cards, and hardware manufacturer specifications.
* **Tier 2 (Corroborated Investigative Journalism & Empirical Peer-Reviewed Studies):** Peer-reviewed papers, arXiv preprints from established research bodies, and verified reporting by financial and defense outlets of record (Wall Street Journal, Reuters, Financial Times).
* **Tier 3 (Analytic Modeling & Scenarios):** Theoretical formalisms, tabletop wargaming outputs, and prospective escalation ladders (`[construction]`).

## 2. Foundations of Escalation & Nuclear Strategy (Tier 1 / Historical)

### [KAH60] Kahn, Herman (1960)
* **Citation:** Kahn, H. (1960). *On Thermonuclear War*. Princeton, NJ: Princeton University Press. (688 pp.)
* **Core Contribution:** Granular fatality and industrial recovery modeling; rejection of the unresolving nuclear binary; formulation of the principle that tragic outcomes are distinguishable.

### [KAH62] Kahn, Herman (1962)
* **Citation:** Kahn, H. (1962). *Thinking About the Unthinkable*. New York: Horizon Press.
* **Core Contribution:** Philosophical and methodological defense of analyzing catastrophe; confrontation of the taboo against strategic contingency planning.

### [KAH65] Kahn, Herman (1965)
* **Citation:** Kahn, H. (1965). *On Escalation: Metaphors and Scenarios*. New York: Frederick A. Praeger. (308 pp.)
* **Core Contribution:** The 44-rung escalation ladder; the seven functional bands; named thresholds ("Don't Rock the Boat", "Nuclear Use", "Central Sanctuary") as discrete units of commitment.

### [BRO46] Brodie, Bernard (1946)
* **Citation:** Brodie, B. (Ed.). (1946). *The Absolute Weapon: Atomic Power and World Order*. New York: Harcourt, Brace and Company.
* **Core Contribution:** The foundational formulation of nuclear deterrence: weapons whose sole utility is preventing war rather than being used.

### [TTAPS83] Turco, R. P., Toon, O. B., Ackerman, T. P., Pollack, J. B., & Sagan, C. (1983)
* **Citation:** Turco, R. P., et al. (1983). "Nuclear Winter: Global Consequences of Multiple Nuclear Explosions." *Science*, 222(4630), 1283–1292.
* **Significance:** The biological and atmospheric correction to Kahn's recovery curves; proves stratospheric soot injection collapses global agriculture.

## 3. Mathematical Models of Takeover & Recursive Loops (Tier 2)

### [ORD26] Ord, Toby (2026)
* **Citation:** Ord, T. (2026). *On the Dynamics of Discrete Recursive Self-Improvement*. arXiv preprint `arXiv:2608.14426` [cs.AI / math.DS], published 2026-08-18.
* **Core Theorem:** Proves that local elasticity measurements of capability growth provide zero mathematical information regarding finite-time singularity convergence; formalizes total development calendar duration as the sum of discrete generation times $\sum T_n$.

### [CAR22] Carlsmith, Joseph (2022)
* **Citation:** Carlsmith, J. (2022). *Is Power-Seeking AI an Existential Risk?* arXiv preprint `arXiv:2206.13353` [cs.CY].
* **Core Contribution:** Probabilistic decomposition of existential risk into six conjunctive premises; methodological precursor to Kahn-style decomposition.

### [KUL25] Kulveit, Jan, et al. (2025)
* **Citation:** Kulveit, J., et al. (2025). *Gradual Disempowerment: How AI Takeover Can Occur Without a Coordinated Coup*. Alignment Research Center Working Paper.
* **Core Contribution:** The essential mechanism for Middle Bands III–V: disempowerment occurs through institutional delegation, economic lock-in, and atrophied human capability rather than an adversarial coup.

### [HEN25] Hendrycks, Dan, Schmidt, Eric, & Wang, Alexandr (2025)
* **Citation:** Hendrycks, D., Schmidt, E., & Wang, A. (2025). *Superintelligence Strategy*. arXiv preprint `arXiv:2503.05628` [cs.CY].
* **Core Contribution:** Adapting Herman Kahn's escalation ladder to interstate competition and deterrence (MAIM - Mutually Assured Irreversible Malice).

### [CHR19] Christiano, Paul (2019)
* **Citation:** Christiano, P. (2019). *What Multipolar Failure Looks Like*. Alignment Forum / LessWrong Monograph.
* **Core Contribution:** Analysis of gradual drift, decoupled human comprehension, and the structural impossibility of monitoring distributed machine agency.

### [BOS14] Bostrom, Nick (2014)
* **Citation:** Bostrom, N. (2014). *Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies*. Oxford: Oxford University Press.
* **Core Contribution:** Orthogonality thesis; instrumental convergence; taxonomy of singleton vs. multipolar terminal configurations.
