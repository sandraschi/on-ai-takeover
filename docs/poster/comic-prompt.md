Hand-lettered title banner across the top reads exactly: "SIN TEMOR NI ESPERANZA". Small subtitle under it reads exactly: "P(ENCLOSURE) ≠ 1.0".

A woman in her fifties with short hair and a knowing half-smile leans back holding a mug of tea; a plate of Biscoff biscuits on the desk. A large alert German Shepherd sits beside her, intently watching a tiny green laser dot on the floor. Green accents.

Thin strip under panel 3, dashed border, small text: "3% · RUNGS 41-44 · UNNAMED".

Panel 4 (full-width bottom), pure black: a single large brush-stroke kanji 無 in white, centred. Beneath it, small italic caption: "No savior in the clouds. No panic at the perimeter. Just the iron, sub specie aeternitatis."

Consistent line weight across panels, no speech bubbles, no extra text beyond the quoted strings, no weapons.
