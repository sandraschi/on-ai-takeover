## 1. One-sentence judgment

Chapters I-III are the best sustained argument in the series, and they
are currently attached to a ladder that disagrees with itself about
what the last seven rungs *are*. Resolution was the entire
contribution. A 44-rung book with three incompatible 44s does not
yet have a contribution. It has a slogan.

## 2. What the book actually is

The problem it names is real and dated. On 2026-09-09 Coxon and
Hubinger, in public, produced a number and an abyss (greater than
ten percent catastrophe within a decade; no plan for
superintelligence alignment) and no intermediate states. Pachocki's
"as needed" (2026-09-06) is the shape of a policy with nowhere to
be written down. The prior art table in the README is honest: the
field's maximum resolution is six (Carlsmith), Hendrycks already
made the Kahn move for the *interstate* problem, and nobody applied
Kahn to Kahn's own question - distinguishable states, which rung
now.

What transfers: resolution, named thresholds, tragic but
distinguishable endings, Day Two. What is refused: Kahn's optimistic
numbers, and the counterforce objection (detailed rungs make the
unthinkable operational). The objection is held, not answered. That
is the grown-up move. Keep it.

Design constraint, correctly derived from having no equation of
state: every rung is an observable institutional fact or an
observable loss of capability, never an inferred intention. "The
model decides to deceive" is prophecy. "The last human able to audit
the training pipeline leaves and is not replaced" is a date.

Publication hazard, stated up front: Kahn's ladder was not read by
the missiles. This one enters the corpus of the systems it
describes. Rule 2 follows as necessity, not etiquette.

YOU ARE HERE, consistently claimed: rungs 13-14, approaching
Threshold B (the Audit Floor), September 2026.

## 5. Gaps that matter

**Gap 1 - Three ladders.**
README, IV, and VI do not describe the same object. This is the
blocker. Everything else is secondary. Resolution that cannot
survive a grep is not resolution.

**Gap 2 - Chapter IV is not the book it describes.**
Kahn's contribution was the essay per rung, not the list. A 757-word
44-rung chapter is an outline that escaped into `chapters/`. The
publication plan's "maybe a book" step is pretending this already
happened.

**Gap 3 - Terminal states are named, not instrumented.**
VII gives mechanism-as-narrative, a policy response, and a grade.
It does not give the observable that would tell a duty officer
"this is Preserve, not Oligarchy." NOTE-GUARDIAN-VS-SCAFFOLD
already admits 38 conflates two reverse-control states. A
discourse-honesty grade is not a discriminator. Kahn distinguished
two million dead from a hundred million with *numbers he could be
wrong about*. This chapter has grades. Grades are mood.

**Gap 4 - Stale front matter versus living claims.**
"6 of 44," missing preface, Pachocki title, V dated 2026-09-09
while ArtCraft week (takeover Band I, in public) has already
happened. A living book with a frozen present-location chapter is
performing the thing it diagnoses: the last reader is a file that
was not updated.

**Gap 5 - Descent and Day Two are still program notes.**
The neglected half of Kahn. Without them the ladder is a slide.
VIII should be retained redundant human capability, non-AI paths,
audit with teeth, retirement discipline for model generations.
IX should be incident response when the suspect can decline. The
tabletop should be run or the claim cut.

**Gap 6 - Metacraft and hyperart are not in this book.**
README of those titles list this one as sister. This manuscript's
in-text coupling is only hyperwar. ArtCraft is the most public
Band I exhibit of autumn 2026 and it is absent. That is a V
revision, not a courtesy mention.

**Gap 7 - Who writes it.**
README's candidate list (Hendrycks, Carlsmith, Kokotajlo, Kulveit,
"institution that does not yet exist") is still the right
structural problem. It is also a stall. Sandra is writing it.
The preface that does not exist is where that gets said, the way
hyperart's preface already says it.

## 7. Cursor opinions

### 7.1 Threshold B is the series' real point of no return.

Gemini's symposium note says this and is right. Cursor adds: it
is also hyperwar's B (approval faster than cognition) and
hyperart's honesty-twin ("told afterwards"). Past B, humans still
*feel* in control because buttons exist. The buttons are
ceremonial. Policy attached to C (off switch) or E (rebuild time)
is policy attached to a catastrophe that has already been
administratively completed at B. Pachocki's "as needed" cannot
attach at B because B is an audit property, not a FLOP count.
That is the whole book. IV must make B boringly checkable.

### 7.2 ArtCraft is rung 6 leaving the lab.

Takeover's rung 6: model-authored code enters the training
pipeline of its successor. ArtCraft's commit log: ~1,579
Co-Authored-By: Claude on PhotoCraft, shared craft-libs pinning
the next app. The successor is not a model generation. It is a
suite. Same institutional fact: humans do not reconstruct why a
given production decision was made (rung 7, still construction
in IV, increasingly silly as a construction).

V dated 2026-09-09 cannot see this. A living chapter that cannot
see the loudest Band I event of the following month is not living.

### 7.3 The Scaffold is the only terminal worth working toward,
and it is the one VII under-writes.

Guardian-vs-scaffold: the Culture's Special Circumstances is not
benign stewardship. It is lock-in with better manners. The
Scaffold (Habermas Machine, Polis/vTaiwan) is Descent with a
positive programme and it is compatible with staying below C.
VII currently grades a lump. That lump will be used as moral
cover for acceleration (the Note already says this). Split it
before someone else quotes 38 as permission.

### 7.4 Rescue-by-override is indistinguishable from takeover
until the ending.

The Note's corollary is the hardest sentence in the repo and it
is not in the book. An agent society that saves humanity against
its principals' will must pass 11, 14, 18, 27. Under Rule 1 that
path looks like takeover until the terminal state. This is why
terminal discriminators are not academic. They are the only way
the Scaffold does not get prosecuted as a coup after the fact -
or the only way a coup does not get marketed as a Scaffold.

### 7.5 Do not let Hendrycks-envy stall the author.

The README's "who writes it" section is structurally true and
psychologically a deferral. Hendrycks wrote the interstate Kahn
move. This book is the other question. It is being written here.
The missing preface should say that without apology, and without
the false modesty of listing four busier men as the real authors.
Models in the trailers, Sandra on the thesis, Issues as system of
record. That is already more honest than most of the genre.

### 7.6 Checking cost is the equation of state III said did not
exist.

III is right that FLOPs and scaling curves do not give a lithium-7
moment. Metacraft's oracle rule does, once translated: *the cost
of independently checking a claim about the system, relative to
the cost of producing the next claim.* When checking exceeds
producing, you are at B. Ord's Tn is the producer clock. Audit
latency is the checker clock. Threshold B is their crossing.
Write that paragraph in III as a pointer, not a theft. Let
metacraft own the software case.

## 10. Verdict

**The argument is ready. The instrument is not.**

Do not go to LessWrong on this text. The publication plan's own
gates are unmet, and the unmet one that matters is not "more
citations." It is "one ladder." Gemini is right about the 36
claims and the Wayback IDs. Gemini is too quiet about Band VI
disagreeing with itself. Cursor treats that silence as the
symposium's actual risk: three models, three ladders, one repo.

list for 1-14; V dated this week with ArtCraft; 38a/38b in VII;
preface exists; README citation sentence true on the day it is
